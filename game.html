Stack a skyscraper floor by floor, fit it with real earthquake engineering, and see if it stands when the ground starts shaking.

  1. Build. A floor swings in from the crane. Tap to drop it. Anything hanging over the edge gets cut off, and narrow floors are weak floors.

  3. Survive. A quake hits, and the physics decides. Keep 60% of your floors standing to reach the next, stronger quake.
